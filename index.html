Kobe's Wins

Mara Quinn is a Los Angeles–based stylist and visual storyteller. Her work lives at the intersection of intuitive dressing, honest character, and image-making with a little tension.

After studying visual merchandising and fashion media, she began assisting on small editorial sets and commercial campaigns—learning to make a point of view feel effortless, even when every detail has been considered.

Today, Mara partners with photographers, artists, and emerging brands on styling, creative direction, and wardrobe worlds that feel specific, human, and quietly memorable.

She is drawn to image-making with texture: clothes that move, locations with a past, and people who look most like themselves when the camera is on.
